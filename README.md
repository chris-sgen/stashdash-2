# stashdash-2

> **Unofficial copy — not the website of Stash Dash.** This repository is a development copy of
> a redesign, published so that the redesign can be looked at in a browser. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by
> Stash Dash. The business's real site is https://www.stashdash.com/.
>
> **A snapshot, not a shop.** The pages were read from the business's site on the evening of October 12, 2026, California time (October 13, between 00:35 and 01:36 UTC), and are
> not kept current. **Nothing can be ordered here:** the buttons that shop ("Shop Online", "Shop Flower" and the like) open the business's own online menu at `www.stashdash.com`. **The subject is cannabis, for adults of 21 and over.**

**Preview: https://chris-sgen.github.io/stashdash-2/**

A **redesign** of **stashdash.com** (Stash Dash, which its own site describes as a family-owned cannabis retailer and delivery service with a dispensary in Atwater Village, Los Angeles), made as a marketing site rather than a store: 71 pages, made by a rebuild pipeline and changed for public hosting as listed under *Hardening*. The live site is an online store; this redesign carries its words and pictures but no products and no product lists. It is a static site with no backend. To look at it, use the preview link. To serve the folder yourself, use a server that answers a folder's address (`/faq/`) with the `index.html` inside it, under the path `/stashdash-2/` (the not-found page names its files there).
Before it shows a page it asks a visitor to confirm being 21 or older (on 69 of the 71 pages; see *Known limits* for what that question does and does not do).

> **11 still pictures and 2 silent video loops in this copy are AI-generated illustrations made for this redesign.**
> They are not photographs of the business, its store, its products or its people. 8 of them show cannabis products of no brand (buds on a dish, pre-rolls, a vape pen, an extract, edibles, a drink, wellness jars, smoking accessories): they head the pages of the 8 categories and stand on their cards (see *Generated imagery*).

Two terms used below. The **private build** is the build of this redesign that this copy was made from,
with its documentation and audit files; it is not published. This **preview** is that build with the
changes listed under *Hardening*.

## Hardening applied to this public copy

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the counts.

1. **`noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, noimageindex` on every page.** This tag is the only index control this copy has. It is a request, not a lock: the large search engines say they follow `noindex`; `noimageindex` is a rule of Google's. A `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /` is also shipped, but it has **no effect** here: crawlers read `robots.txt` only at the host root (`chris-sgen.github.io/robots.txt`, which only a separate repository for that host could provide), not under `/stashdash-2/`. GitHub Pages cannot send an `X-Robots-Tag` header either. That leaves every file that is not a page (the pictures, the loops, the fonts, the stylesheets, the script, the licence texts and these documents) with no index control of its own; on this site they are linked only from pages marked `nofollow`. The repository itself is public: github.com lists every file of it, and its page there can be indexed.
2. **Link-preview tags.** `og:url` points at this preview, `og:description` / `twitter:description` carry the disclosure, `og:site_name` reads "Unofficial preview (not Stash Dash)", and `og:title` / `twitter:title` begin "Unofficial preview:" (71 pages), and the business's name is taken off where it stands apart at the start or the end of the title ("Stash Dash | …", "… | Stash Dash") (it stays inside 4 card titles that speak of the business by name: `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/events/`, `/locations/`). The picture tags (`og:image`, `twitter:image`: 186 tags) are removed and `twitter:card` is `summary`, the small card, so no tag offers a picture. The business's mark is not this copy's tab or home-screen icon (142 icon links removed; an empty icon stands in). **The `<title>` of a page is not marked**: it is the private build's title, and most titles name the business. What a link card shows depends on the app: apps that take a link's description or site name from these tags show the disclosure; apps that read none of these tags show the page's own title with the `chris-sgen.github.io` address; apps that pick a picture from the page itself may show one of its pictures.
3. **JSON-LD removed** (70 blocks). It asserted, as data for search engines, the business's identity, its address, opening hours and telephone number, and the trail of each page.
4. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` on every page, so a click on an outbound link does not tell its target this address. The map of the store is a keyless Google Maps frame that the page's script puts in place only when a visitor asks for it (68 pages offer it); in the private build the frame asks to send the page's address with its request, and here it asks to send none (a policy on a frame overrules the page's). No page holds a frame of its own, and no page asks another host for a file.
5. **No on-page disclosure banner.** The disclosure is carried by the link-preview tags (in apps that show them; see 2) and by this README. Apart from the notes named here a page looks like a page of the business: its logo, its licence number, its address, telephone number and support address, its copyright line and its words. 11 pages carry a note of this preview under their title, before their buttons: `/privacy-policy/`, `/terms/` say that the text of the document is the live site's and describes the live service; `/contact/`, `/deals/`, `/delivery/`, `/faq/`, `/gift-cards/`, `/learn/order-help-returns-and-exchanges/`, `/learn/paying-for-cannabis-delivery/`, `/locations/atwater-village/` say that their answers are the live site's own words about its service (accounts, orders, payments, chat, delivery), which this copy does not have, that its Shop buttons open the business's own online menu and that its Call and Email links reach the business; `/events/` says that the flyer is the business's as its site showed it on October 12, 2026. Every note adds that this preview is a separate redesign; none says "unofficial".
6. `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` are not shipped, because they advertise the business's real addresses to crawlers. The `_redirects` and `.htaccess` files are not shipped either, since GitHub Pages ignores them: the 25 forwards of the private build from the live site's addresses to this redesign's (`/aboutus` to `/about/`, `/termsandconditions` to `/terms/`, …) do not exist here, and such an address shows the not-found page.
7. **`<link rel="canonical">` names the live address of the page.** In the private build every page names its own future address on `www.stashdash.com`, which the live site does not answer yet. Here 62 pages name the address at which the live site shows the same page today, some of them spelled otherwise (`/about/` names `/aboutus`, `/categories/edibles/` names `/categories/Edibles`, `/deals/` names `/shop/daily-deals`); `/brands/cat4/` names `/brands/cat4` (the live page at `/brands/CAT4` names the lower-case `/brands/cat4` as its canonical); that is the usual signal for a duplicate, and deliberately different from `og:url`. 9 have none: `/404.html`, `/categories/`, `/learn/`, `/learn/california-cannabis-purchase-limits/`, `/learn/order-help-returns-and-exchanges/`, `/learn/paying-for-cannabis-delivery/`, `/learn/what-you-need-for-cannabis-delivery/`, `/locations/`, `/locations/atwater-village/` (pages the live site does not have; it answers `/categories` with its not-found page). **Description tags** are the private build's, except on `/events/`, where the disclosure stands instead: the private build's description restated the flyer's date and offers, whose typed-out words this copy does not carry (see 9).
8. **The age question asks for a confirmation of age and for no agreement.** On the live site, and in the private build, the question also has a box to tick, "I agree to Stash Dash's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy". A copy that is not the business's asks nobody to agree to anything: the box, its two links and the refusal shown without it are taken out on all 69 pages that carry the question, and the question keeps the end of the live sentence: "You must be 21+ years of age to visit this site." **Its answer is kept to this copy:** "yes" is noted in the browser's local storage under the name `stashdash2_preview_age`, so that an answer given on another project of the `chris-sgen.github.io` host does not answer for this one (another preview of the same business on that host keeps its answer under another name).
9. **Text of the private build that this copy does not carry.** On `/events/` the words of the event flyer set as text, which the private build prints beside the flyer for readers who cannot see the picture: typed out they are an invitation with a date, a street address, a telephone number, offers and other businesses' names that this copy cannot keep current. The flyer itself is shown, as on the live page, and its description for screen readers still gives the party's name and day.
10. **Marks taken off links:** 1 link (`/terms/`, to `google.com`: `entry` and `source`, marks Google Maps puts on an address shared from an e-mail app). No link of this copy carries a campaign mark or a referral code.
11. **Files left out, files added.** 28 files of the private build that no page, stylesheet or script of this copy names are not shipped: sizes of pictures that only the link-preview tags, the structured data and the icon links named (among them the business's icon, the picture its link previews showed, and the larger sizes of brand marks). The comments of the stylesheet and of the script, notes of the private build to whoever maintains it, are taken out (7 and 18 comments); the script was checked to parse without them. Added: `assets/css/preview.css` (the styles of the notes and of the changed age question; on the pages of the two documents, the title and its note set in the centred column of the text, and on `/events/` centred over the flyer; and the flyer, which stands alone here, set wider than the private build sets it beside its words), the licence texts of the 3 typefaces beside the font files, `.nojekyll` (GitHub Pages serves the files as they are) and `.gitattributes` (no line endings are rewritten).
12. `404.html` names its files under `/stashdash-2/`, because GitHub Pages serves it for an unknown address at any depth.

## Known limits

- **Nothing can be ordered here.** The cart, the checkout, the sign-in, the address check, the gift-card purchase and the newsletter sign-up are on the business's own online menu, which the menu buttons open (see *Links that lead to live services* below). This copy has no accounts and no forms. Its pages set no cookie and keep nothing but the answer to the age question. The host, GitHub Pages, receives every request as any web host does and keeps its own logs.
- **The age question is a click-through.** It reads "You must be 21+ years of age to visit this site." and takes "I'm at least 21 years old" or "Exit this site" (69 pages; the two documents of the live site, `/terms/` and `/privacy-policy/`, have none). It checks no document and verifies nothing. The page is loaded behind it, dimmed and not usable until it is answered. With scripts off it is still asked, on every page anew, and an address that ends in `#gate-passed` shows the page without it. The pictures and the loops can be opened by their own addresses without the question, and every file can be read in the repository.
- **This is a snapshot, taken on the evening of October 12, 2026, California time (October 13, between 00:35 and 01:36 UTC).** 11 of the live pages were read again on October 14, 2026 (California time); the text of 9 of them had not changed, and this copy's list of all brands follows the second reading. The menu fees ("$50 minimum order. $3.50 delivery fee on all orders.") stand at the top of every page as the live site showed them then. The business's own site is the place for what holds today.
- **The words written for this redesign speak for the business, and it did not write them.** See the second row of the first table: the titles, headings, introductions and labels say "our" and "we" in the business's name. They restate what the live site says, but their wording, and every statement that puts two of the live site's facts together, is this redesign's.
- **The opening hours are not confirmed.** "7am–10pm daily" stands on every page (71) (at the top and in the footer of every page, and in the weekly table of `/locations/atwater-village/`) and in the description tags of `/contact/`, `/locations/atwater-village/`, `/locations/`, written as the business's own statement. It is read from the line of store details on the business's flyer for one Saturday's event ("7am-10pm Daily"); the business has not confirmed these as its regular hours, and no page says so.
- **The answers on the pages are the live site's, and many of them speak about the live site.** The pages named in Hardening 5 carry a note. Elsewhere the business's words are carried as written, without a note: the home page carries answers of the FAQ, one of which points to the live site's chat ("the chat icon on the bottom right of the website"; the line after it says that the chat is on the online menu); 5 of the 8 category pages (`/categories/accessories/`, `/categories/extracts/`, `/categories/flower/`, `/categories/pre-rolls/`, `/categories/vapes/`) carry answers about returns or purchase limits, and two guides answers about ID, delivery and limits. Offers on the pages (the gift card's "Credits never expire!", the menu fees) are the business's, as its site stated them on the day of the snapshot. This copy takes no orders and delivers nothing.
- **The event on `/events/` has a date and will pass.** The flyer is a picture; it shows the business's street address, telephone number and social-media name, the names of two bakeries and what is offered to the first customers in line. Its day also stands in the flyer's description for screen readers. The page's note gives the day the flyer was read.
- **The generated pictures and loops are illustrations, not the business's photographs.** No caption or frame on the pages tells them apart; `PROVENANCE.md` lists each one. The category pictures show cannabis products: they are not products of the business or of any brand it sells.
- **The video loops play by themselves** on 2 pages (`/delivery/`, `/`); each has a button to pause it. They are silent and hidden from screen readers. When the device asks for reduced motion, or saves data, they do not play at all, and the still picture shows instead.
- **Links that lead to live services.** 692 links on 71 pages lead to the business's online menu on `www.stashdash.com` (the most frequent: "Shop Online" 150, "Shop the menu" 74, "Subscribe" 71, "Shop This Location" 68, "Shop" 22, "Open the online menu" 22).
