Age, ID and medical use

How old do I have to be to order cannabis online?

For medical patients, you must be at least 18 years old and have a valid state-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport, along with your original stamped doctor's recommendation. For recreational consumers, you must be at least 21 years old and have a valid state-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport.

Are any medical recommendations required?

Medical recommendations are required for customers under the age of 21 or for those of legal age (21) who wish to surpass the daily sales limit set by California Cannabis Law.

Do I need a medical marijuana card to get cannabis delivered?

No, a medical marijuana card is not required. Stash Dash delivers recreational cannabis to adults aged 21 and over, as long as they present a valid state-issued photo ID.

What kind of ID can I use to place an order?

Acceptable forms of ID include: non-expired government-issued photo ID card (such as a driver's license), military photo ID card, or passport.

Can I use an expired ID?

No. All IDs must be up to date and valid. If your ID or driver's license is expired, we can also accept other forms of ID, such as a government-issued photo ID card, military photo ID card, and passport.

Can I show a photo of my ID if I do not have the physical card?

Due to California state law, customers are required to show our drivers a physical and valid ID upon delivery that matches the name on the order. Failure to show a physical and valid ID upon delivery will result in a cancellation.

Delivery

What do I need to get weed delivered from Stash Dash Delivery?

Medical patients must be 18 years or older and present a valid, state-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport, along with their original, stamped doctor's recommendation. Recreational consumers must be 21 years or older and provide a valid, state-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport.

Do I need to be present when the cannabis delivery driver arrives with my order?

Yes, you must be present to receive your delivery and provide a state-issued photo ID. Additionally, medical patients will need to present their original stamped recommendation.

Why haven't I received an ETA?

ETA notifications are sent out via text or voice as soon as your order is first in line on our driver's queue. Processing may take a little longer as we gather your requested items and assign them to our driver. If delays persist, please reach out to us using the chat feature on the bottom right of the website for further assistance. We will do our best to provide you with an accurate ETA!

Why is my order still processing?

Your order may be taking longer than usual due to a high demand for orders we might be experiencing at the moment. Additionally, the ETA may be slightly extended as our system optimizes a more efficient route for your driver. If delays persist, please reach out to us via the chat feature on the bottom right of the website!

Why did my ETA change during delivery?

We appreciate your patience as we strive to provide you with the most accurate ETA for your order. Please note that ETA changes may occur due to various factors, including traffic conditions, previous order complications, weather conditions, and our system's efforts to optimize the most efficient route for deliveries. If you require the most up-to-date ETA for your order, we recommend calling our store number to speak with a representative. Our team will be more than happy to assist you and provide you with the latest information regarding your delivery.

Can I change my address once it's been placed?

If you need to update your address, we kindly ask you to contact our customer support team as soon as possible to receive assistance. Our team will be more than happy to help you with the address update process. Please be aware that updating the address may require adjustments to your order, as our drivers are allocated based on specific locations. Rest assured, we will do our best to accommodate your request and ensure a smooth delivery experience for you.

Can I delay my order?

If you placed an order and there is need for you to postpone, please contact us and we can reschedule your delivery time to match your preferred delivery time.

Purchase limits

How much can I buy per day? (Recreational)

We adhere to the state-mandated limits on purchase quantities. Adult-use consumers may buy up to 28.5 grams of flower and 8 grams of cannabis concentrate

How much can I buy per day? (Medical-Use)

We adhere to the state-mandated limits on purchase quantities. Medical marijuana patients are permitted to purchase up to 8 ounces of medical cannabis per day.

Are there any restrictions or limits on delivery orders?

We adhere to the state-mandated limits on purchase quantities. Adult-use consumers may buy up to 28.5 grams of flower and 8 grams of cannabis concentrate. Medical marijuana patients are permitted to purchase up to 8 ounces of medical cannabis per day.

Problems, returns and exchanges

I have a problem with my order, who should I contact?

Please reach out to us on the chat feature on the bottom right of the website, and one of our representatives will be available to further assist you. If the order you received was within the last 72 hours, you can also get a quicker resolution via our Automated Customer Service - just log in, click on the person icon on the top left of the website, and click "My Orders". Then locate the problematic order and click "Were there issues with your order?" and follow the prompts for immediate assistance!

Who do I contact if I've received a damaged order?

If your order was delivered in less than 72hrs, you can report an issue through our automated system when logged into your account. If your order was delivered more than 72hrs ago, please reach out to us via the chat feature on the bottom right of the website. One of our representatives will be available to further assist you.

Do you take returns?

All cannabis product sales are final. We do not exchange products when customers do not like the look, feel, taste, smell or any other characteristic of the product. We only consider exchanges when a hardware product, such as a battery, pod, or cart are damaged or defective. All exchanges shall be at the discretion of Stash Dash. Refunds are not permitted.
